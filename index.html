01 — frame

Deadcat, drawn from scratch.

The frame uses a 7-inch deadcat layout. The front arms swing wide so the camera gets a clear view with no props in the shot. I designed the arms, plates, and battery and Raspberry Pi mounts in Autodesk Inventor, then 3D-printed and refined them one part at a time.

02 — flight controller

Kakute H7, rescued.

The drone started on INAV. I moved it to ArduPilot for real mission planning, but partway through the switch the USB-C connector tore off the board. I flashed the firmware anyway through STM32 DFU mode, and now every setting and calibration happens over the air.

03 — companion computer

A Pi that talks for it.

A Raspberry Pi 4B rides along as a companion computer. It bridges the flight controller's serial port to WiFi with mavlink-router and runs its own hotspot, so Mission Planner and my own ground-station app connect wirelessly. A Pi Camera Module 3 handles the imaging.

  1. Kakute H7
  2. UART
  3. Pi 4B · mavlink-router
  4. WiFi hotspot
  5. Mission Planner / drone-gcs
